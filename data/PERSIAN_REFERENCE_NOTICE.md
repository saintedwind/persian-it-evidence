# Persian public reference extracts

The six files in persian-reference/ are introductory plaintext extracts from Persian Wikipedia, retrieved through MediaWiki on 2026-10-14. Text authors: Persian Wikipedia contributors. Each file's title, immutable revision URL, contributor history link and SHA-256 are recorded in reference-manifest.json.
